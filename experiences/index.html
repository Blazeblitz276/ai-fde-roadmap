Experiences

The roadmap covers concepts. This section is where they get used: real projects written up as a decision journal. Each entry records the context, the options, the decision, the evidence behind it, what it implies, and the FDE skill it taught, linked back to the matching roadmap topic.

How to read an experience
Every decision follows the same shape: context → options → decision → basis → implication → FDE skill learned. Projects are written from public information only. Where earlier work informed a choice, only the general lesson is kept, never the figures or internals.
